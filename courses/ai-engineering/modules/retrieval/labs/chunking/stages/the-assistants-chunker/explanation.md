The four checks hold the chunker to every page the help desk has: every word kept, in order; no chunk past its size; every chunk the page's own text; every chunk at its own place. None of the problems showed on the page in each of the assistant's tests: no block joined another, no page ended with blocks being joined, and none held the same text twice.

A chunker that keeps text, rather than places, has to find each chunk in its page again, which only works while the chunk is the page's text, character for character, and is searched for after the chunk before it.
