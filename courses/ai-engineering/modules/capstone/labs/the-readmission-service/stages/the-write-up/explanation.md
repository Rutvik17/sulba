The write-up is the service's record: what it learned from, how well it ranks, how far its probabilities hold, what it calls, and where it stops. Each number is worked out from the model and the stays as the page is written, so a model trained again on a new extract gets a page that matches it, and no number on it goes stale.

The page gives its uncertainty where it has it: the AUC with its standard error, and the probabilities set against what happened to the same stays. It names the comparison that makes the AUC mean something, ranking by the year's inpatient visits alone. And it says what the model doesn't know, since the stays it learned from set the patients it can answer for.

Downloaded, the project runs the service on your machine with uv. `README.md` gives the commands: train and save the model, run your tests, and start the service.
