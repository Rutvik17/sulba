The assistant's tests sorted the validation beans as one batch, which holds every variety in about the training beans' proportions, so its statistics were close to theirs, and they checked the estimate only for being a share. The checks used the line the way the company will: a sack of one variety, an estimate it will quote, votes that tie, and a bean the camera half missed.

---

The statistics that scaled the training beans are part of the model, as much as the beans themselves: every bean the line sees after is scaled by them, whatever batch it comes in. And a model can't be measured on the beans it holds, since each of them is its own nearest neighbour.
