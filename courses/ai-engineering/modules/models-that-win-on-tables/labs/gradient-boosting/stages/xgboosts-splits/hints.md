`share - said_yes` works with `said_yes` as booleans, which count as 1 and 0.

The three terms of the gain share one form, a sum of gradients squared over a sum of hessians plus `lam`, for the left rows, the rest, and all of them.

`np.unique(X[:, column])` gives a column's distinct values from smallest. With the first left out, every threshold leaves at least one row on each side; compare the gains with a strict `>` to keep the first of equal ones.
