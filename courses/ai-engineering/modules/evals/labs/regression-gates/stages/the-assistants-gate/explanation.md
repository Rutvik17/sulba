The four checks hold the gate to the help desk's own releases: every reply counted, each question's difference taken, a rerun let through, and each page's questions counted together. None of the problems showed in the assistant's tests. They gave it a release that cites nothing, which any gate stops, and the baseline against a copy of itself, whose change is exactly zero, so no test needed the samples, the pairs, the pages or the margin to come out right.

Two of the problems made the gate blunter: a first reply alone and a comparison of the two scores alone both widen the margin, so a drop has to be larger before the gate finds it. The other two made it stop releases that are no worse: stopping on any drop stops about half of all reruns, and a margin that counts each question alone is too narrow for a change that moves a page's questions together.
