A move is two writes, a booking and a cancellation, and the caller must never be left between them with no call. Booking the new time first means a failure leaves the old call in place: the caller has two calls for an instant, never none. Checking the new time before either write means the usual failure, a time taken since the proposal, writes nothing at all.

The move is a proposal like any other: the desk looks up the call and a free time before the caller is asked, keeps the move until the caller's next message, and makes it only on a yes. The model can't move a call by itself, since no tool it can call writes. Each refusal names what the caller can ask for instead, their booked calls or the times that are free, so the model's next question can be one the desk can answer.
