Each problem passed the assistant's tests because each test checked the end of a conversation that went to plan. The desk's state after a yes was right, whether the call had been booked before the yes or after it; no test said no, or let a time go between the proposal and the yes, or gave the caller two tickets for one service.

A write that waits for a yes has to wait in every path. Holding the call "for the caller now" is a write the caller never agreed to, and one that takes the time from everyone else. A proposal that outlives a no can be made by a yes meant for something else. And a yes is checked against the desk as it is when the yes comes, not as it was when the call was proposed: anything the desk can't finish, it doesn't start.

The two tickets were the model's question to ask, not the code's to guess. The desk lists them in the error, and the model asks the caller which.
