Write `medians`:

```
medians(numbers) -> the median of the numbers so far, after each one, as floats
```

`medians([5, 15, 1, 3])` returns `[5.0, 10.0, 5.0, 4.0]`: the middle number of an odd count, and the mean of the middle two of an even count. The numbers are whole numbers, and the tests' longest list has {% $fig.numbers %}.
