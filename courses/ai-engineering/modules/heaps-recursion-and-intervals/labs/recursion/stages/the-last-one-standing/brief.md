People numbered 1 to `people` stand in a circle. Counting from person 1, every `step`th person leaves the circle, and the counting goes on from the next person still in it. Write `survivor`:

```
survivor(people, step) -> the number of the last person left
```

`survivor(5, 2)` returns 3: people 2, 4, 1 and 5 leave, in that order. The tests' largest circle has {% $fig.people %} people.
