The pseudonym is keyed. A plain hash of the subject would hide it only from someone without the list of the office's staff: hashing every subject on the list and matching would name everyone in the log. Under the office's key, the same person has the same pseudonym from one line to the next, so the auditors can follow what one person searched, and only the office, holding the key, can say who that is.

The line holds what the auditors need and nothing more. The question stays out because it holds what a caller told the office, which belongs to the caller's case, not to a log of who searched. The documents are their ids, so the line says what was shown without copying it.

Times are in UTC, to the second, and a time without a zone is refused rather than guessed at, so lines from machines in different zones line up.
