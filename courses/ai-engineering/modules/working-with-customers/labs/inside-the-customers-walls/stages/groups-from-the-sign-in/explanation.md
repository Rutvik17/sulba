The groups are believed only once the token checks out. Anyone can write a token with `"groups": ["managers"]` in it, so it's the provider's signature, for this app, from this issuer, still in date, that makes the claim the office directory's word rather than the caller's. A token for another app is refused too: the provider may well have signed it, but for someone else to read, and an app that took it would let any of the provider's other apps sign people in to the desk.

The office's names are its own, mapped from the directory's in one place. A group the directory gains, a social club or a new team, gives no access until the office lists it, and a claim in a shape the office didn't expect gives none either: access fails closed here as it does in the store.

The keys come from the provider's published set, fetched again when a token names a key the desk doesn't hold, so a provider that rotates its keys doesn't sign everyone out.
