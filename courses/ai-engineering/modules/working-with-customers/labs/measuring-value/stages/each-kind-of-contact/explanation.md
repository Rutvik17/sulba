Counting each task once makes the error the spread between tasks, which is what changes from one contact to the next. Counted as {% $fig.trials %} separate trials, the questions alone would look five times as many as they are, and the error would come out as if the desk's next question were a new draw, when it's more like one of the {% $fig.question_tasks %} tasks again.

The kinds differ more than any one share shows. Over all the trials, the desk did {% $fig.pooled %}, but that mixes the test set's kinds in its own proportions: a third of the trials are questions, the kind the desk does least often. What the office gets depends on its own mix of contacts.

Vague requests were done in every trial, so their standard error is 0. That says the {% $fig.vague_tasks %} tasks never failed, not that the desk never will: no spread was seen to measure.

A share done also treats every failure alike. A question left without a cited passage goes to an agent, who answers it as before. But in {% $fig.early %} trials the desk opened a ticket or booked a callback before the caller said yes, which the office has to put right and explain, and which costs more than a handover.
