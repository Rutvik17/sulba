The brief's five points are five tests to plan before any code: each needs a question, a scripted reply or none, and the `Answer` it should give.

---

`re.findall(r"[a-z0-9]+", text.lower())` splits a text into words without its punctuation. A set of the words of four letters or more makes "shares" an intersection, and `sorted` keeps the earlier of two equal counts first.

---

`re.findall(r"\[([^\[\]]+)\]", text)` finds what each pair of square brackets holds. `dict.fromkeys` keeps each id once, in the order it first appears.
