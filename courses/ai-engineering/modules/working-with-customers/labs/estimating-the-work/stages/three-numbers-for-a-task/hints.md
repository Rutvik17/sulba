The density's area is 1, and its peak is at `likely`. The mean of a triangle's distribution is the average of its three corners.

---

Solve `F(d) = q` on each side of `likely`. Which side `d` is on depends on whether `q` is below `F(likely)`, which is `(likely - low) / (high - low)`.

---

On the left, `(d - low)^2 = q (high - low)(likely - low)`, so `d = low + sqrt(...)`. On the right, `(high - d)^2 = (1 - q)(high - low)(high - likely)`.
