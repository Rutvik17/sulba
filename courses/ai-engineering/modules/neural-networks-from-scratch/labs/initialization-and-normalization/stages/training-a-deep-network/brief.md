Ship the office's deep network. `make_layers(widths, rng)` returns its layers in order: a linear layer between each pair of widths, a ReLU after each linear layer but the last, and whatever else it needs to train, drawing every random number from `rng`. The starter's `make_layers` builds the network stage 1 began with, started as `torch.nn.Linear` starts, which never learned.

The office trains it as stage 1 did: {% $fig.hidden %} hidden layers of {% $fig.width %}, and {% $fig.steps %} steps of SGD at a learning rate of {% $fig.lr %}, on batches of {% $fig.batch %} training digits. Each step runs `forward(X, training=True)` through the layers and `backward` back through them, then moves each array in a layer's `params()` against its gradient in `grads()`. To read digits, it runs `forward(X, training=False)`, on many digits at once or on one.

Every layer follows that protocol, as the starter's `Linear` and `ReLU` do, and your `BatchNorm1d` and `LayerNorm` from stages 2 and 3: import them from `norms.py`, and your starts from `init.py`. Choose on the {% $fig.validation %} validation digits, which come from the same people as the {% $fig.training %} training digits. The office reads people it has never seen, and so do the checks: they read the {% $fig.test %} test digits, written by others.
