The desk trusts the college's provider for who someone is, and only the office's directory for which student they are. A student's id never comes from the request: the session cookie names a person the provider signed in, and `directory` maps their email to the record the office holds for them. Someone the college signs in who isn't one of the office's students, such as a member of staff, gets nothing from the desk.

A reply holds its pool connection from the question to the verdict, for the search, the record and the ticket, so the pool's size bounds how many replies run at once, and a question past it waits for a connection to come back.

As the help desk did, the desk refuses a request from another site that would change something, whatever cookies the browser sends with it.
