The model cites passages by number, and a student reads pages, so `cited` turns each number into its passage's page. `find` gives each page once, so each number names a page of its own. A number beyond the passages is one the model made up, and `cited` leaves it out.

A reply that cites nothing isn't one a student can check against the office's pages, so it goes to staff. So does a reply that says `I don't know`, which the instructions ask the model to give when the passages don't answer the question, even beside an answer: it's the model's own word that the passages may not hold one. So does a request that fails: a student is better served by a reply from staff than by an error.

The search turns away a question the pages don't answer before the model sees it: no request, no tokens, and no answer from passages that don't hold one.
