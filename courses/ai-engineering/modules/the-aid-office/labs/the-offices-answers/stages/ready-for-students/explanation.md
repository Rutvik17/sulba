A student's aid comes from their record, read for the student signed in, and never from the model: the record is the answer, and a model that reworded it could get a number wrong. The question's text can name anyone; the record read is the signed-in student's.

Which questions are about a student's own aid is the student's to say, with the page's button, rather than the model's to guess. A wrong guess either sends an aid question to the office's pages, which can't answer it, or reads a record for a question that wasn't about one.

A ticket keeps who asked what, for the office's staff, who reply by email. A visitor who isn't signed in has no address to reply to, so the desk asks them to sign in rather than keep a question no one can answer.

The full build goes on from here: the desk behind a web API with sign-in, its replies streamed to the chat page, in a container that runs offline on the office's server.
