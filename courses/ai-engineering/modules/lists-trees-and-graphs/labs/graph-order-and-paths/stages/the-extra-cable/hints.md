Add the cables one at a time. Which cable is the first to join two computers that are already joined some other way, and why is it the answer?

---

Keep the computers in groups that are joined, each group named by one of its computers. A cable whose ends are in one group closes the loop; otherwise it merges their groups. To find a computer's group quickly, follow its links to the group's name, and shorten the path as you go.
